## 📱 About agriSense ai Mobile

**agriSense ai** is an all-in-one mobile solution designed for modern farmers, agronomists, and agricultural enterprise managers. Powered by artificial intelligence and real-time field tracking, it brings farm management, crop health diagnosis, and operational planning directly to your Android device.

---

## ⬇️ Download & Installation

### How to Install on Android

1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** section of this repository.
2. Download the latest `.apk` asset file (e.g., `agriSense-ai-v1.0.0.apk`).
3. Tap the downloaded file on your Android device.
4. If prompted, enable **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser or file manager:
   - Go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Special App Access** > **Install Unknown Apps** > Allow for your browser/file manager.
5. Tap **Install** and launch **agriSense ai**.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 AI-Powered Crop Disease Detection
- **Instant Camera Diagnosis**: Point your device camera or upload photos of leaves and crops to diagnose diseases instantly.
- **Smart Treatment Recommendations**: Receive treatment and preventive care recommendations immediately.
- **Historical Diagnosis Logs**: Maintain a timeline of plant health issues across your fields.

### 🌱 Crop Lifecycle & Field Management
- **End-to-End Tracking**: Record crop types, planting dates, growth stages, and expected harvest timelines.
- **Field & Plot Mapping**: Visualize farm boundaries and manage individual plots directly from the mobile map interface.
- **Crop Lifecycle Updates**: Quickly edit, inspect, and update crop statuses while in the field.

### 🌦️ Real-Time Agricultural Weather
- **Field Weather Forecasts**: Accurate temperature, humidity, rainfall probability, and wind metrics.
- **Agricultural Advisory**: Weather-driven suggestions for irrigation, spraying, and harvesting windows.

### 📦 Inventory & Harvest Logging
- **Stock Tracking**: Monitor seeds, fertilizers, chemical treatments, tools, and machinery in real-time.
- **Harvest Yields**: Log harvest dates, batch identifiers, quantities, and storage locations straight from the field.
- **Low Stock Notifications**: Avoid shortages during critical farming cycles.

### 👥 Team, Tasks & Attendance
- **Daily Task Planner**: Assign, track, and complete daily farm chores and maintenance workflows.
- **Worker & Farmer Directory**: Maintain worker records and manage field personnel.
- **Attendance Records**: Log and verify workforce presence directly on-site.

### 📊 Visual Analytics & Reports
- **Interactive Dashboard**: Gain bird's-eye visibility over farm productivity, inventory health, and open tasks.
- **Visual Trends**: Charts and metrics to analyze yields, treatment success, and resource utilization.

### 🌐 Multilingual & Accessible
- Designed for field workers with support for multiple languages and an intuitive, touch-friendly mobile interface.
